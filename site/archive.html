ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

A world made from elsewhere.

Las Vegas gathers fragments of other places: a Parisian tower, an Egyptian pyramid, a New York skyline. Buildings, signs and spectacles are copied, staged and brought together in the desert.

This archive follows the cultural life of the copy. The “fake” becomes material for a new world, with its own sounds, images, rituals and imagination. Borrowed forms take on new meanings; imitation becomes part of the culture of Las Vegas.

The V&A visit brings another setting into the archive: sculptural displays, architectural fragments, gallery spaces and the sounds of people moving among them. These records invite comparison with Las Vegas through shared forms and subjects, while keeping each collection’s context distinct.

Follow a subject across photographs, films, recordings and models. Search a landmark, a symbol or a sound to trace how these borrowed worlds are represented, experienced and reimagined.

About the material

Cultural references and source associations do not establish an image or recording's physical location. Unverified locations remain unverified.

Audio descriptions and tags are AI drafts awaiting human listening review. Video descriptions combine supplied source context and sampled visual annotations; automated predictions can be incorrect. Source links, creators and stated license information are retained with each record.

Image downloads are web copies. Audio and video downloads include MP3, M4A and MP4 clips. One damaged V&A video is presented as its recovered playable fragment. Model downloads include Blender files, GLB previews and original PLY scans; Gaussian scans have a compact interactive splat preview. Some models are simplified geometry or shallow photo references. A keyword search does not make an AI call.
