# Exhibition experiment (independent test branch)

In Exhibition Walk, arrange saved models in the 20 x 20 floor grid, then click **Make space**. Each measured footprint becomes a support at the same point; its relative source height controls a taller support (7.2 m plus half the measured source height). Real mesh sections control taper, bulges and offsets; detected long protrusions become upward branches. Lower-heavy sources may invert their section profile into top-heavy growth. Smooth fillets join trunks, branches and a continuous roof along a spanning tree, so distant points remain connected. The roof is a closed solid with approximately 0.24 m vertical thickness. Actual material appearances are rendered against transparency, and large foreground-only patches are placed with mild rotation and less than 1% positional warp; only narrow borders blend. This preserves recognisable motif fragments without cutout margins. Source palettes stay distinct without retaining cutout margins. The same world coordinates map each source texture continuously across its trunk, branches and canopy region. This is procedural geometry, not AI architectural reconstruction, and does not reproduce the reference photograph's perforations.

**Enter space** chooses a clear spot under the roof and restores WASD navigation. Supports constrain movement. All generated canopies maintain at least 5 m of clear circulation height outside the support plots. Branches start at or above 6 m, with their radii accounted for. The white gallery starts at 12 m high and extends automatically to accommodate each canopy. Axonometric framing includes the taller roof and the floor, and the original yellow footprints remain visible through the space. **Original models** restores the editable exhibition; edits mark the previous space out of date. **Export GLB** downloads geometry and embedded appearance textures. Original placements are never replaced. A small local recipe rebuilds the active preview after refresh.

The first agent has a 0-50 model slider. It runs one local generation at a time, randomly chooses the Las Vegas-to-V&A or V&A-to-Las-Vegas workflow, and automatically saves completed models in the floating workspace library and the existing saved gallery. **Stop** finishes/saves the current job and skips queued jobs. Missing local generation capability leaves an editable collage; it does not report that models were generated. No new hosted service or paid API is introduced.

The second agent imports up to 50 library models only when the floor is empty. When exhibits already exist, it rearranges those exact placements without adding or removing any model. Large varied square footprints are packed tightly near the axonometric camera target with one full white cell between every pair, including diagonal neighbours. Scale stays near the footprint size (90-99%) and rotation varies. A whole proposed layout is validated and applied atomically. Up to 100 one-cell placements can fit with the white gap; an impossible collection leaves the current layout intact. Library contents and floor layouts remain private to this browser/device.

Audio uses varied credible metadata matches; when these are weak or exhausted it falls back to a random archive audio, avoiding the most recent 24 choices where possible. The basis is recorded in linkedMedia. Existing version-1 saved associations are upgraded once on opening while preserving their video, saved camera, model bytes and group. Videos stay muted.

Validation: npm run build; py -3.14 scripts/check_project.py; node qa/verify_canopy_geometry.mjs; node qa/verify_canopy_growth.mjs. The geometry audit checks a single welded closed connected surface for distant supports, finite attributes, retained sizes and unchanged original buffers/materials. Browser validation uses isolated test data and intercepted generation responses with actual GLB fixtures, without running AI inference, to check batch count/directions, saving, stop, layout, audio diversity/migration, walking, texture export and mobile width.

The accepted shared main and the public site are unchanged. This experiment stays on work/chenyiwei/exhibition-testing until reviewed.
