# Exhibition experiment (independent test branch)

In Exhibition Walk, arrange saved models in the 40 x 40 m floor with 20 x 20 indexed cells, then click **Make space**. Each measured footprint becomes a support at the same point. Generated support height is 2.8 + 0.40 h + 0.008 h squared metres, where h is measured source height; minimum circulation clearance is 2 m outside support plots. Real mesh cross-sections control taper and offsets. Upper corners and protrusions seed curved stems that split into two limbs, with a narrow central crown and smooth implicit unions joining the roof. The mesh has one continuous closed surface and approximately 0.60 m roof thickness; no source mesh is overwritten. Each spanning-tree bridge adds a smooth arched rise of up to 8 m, producing a curved top even between similar-height anchors. Column sections and curved limbs are thinner; branch roots derive directly from source protrusions without a fixed lower height clamp. Sixteen-segment curved limbs and rounded roof edges join through smooth unions without a global top clipping plane. Low branches are contained inside support plots, while exterior circulation keeps 2 m clearance. It is procedural geometry rather than AI architectural reconstruction.

Material generation builds a unique 1024 px texture from very small overlapping source-texture fragments with least-error seam paths. Capture uses the source base maps without display lighting. Fine weathering and contrasting source pigments are retained. A narrow seam mask blends neighbouring texels only at patch joins, softening boundaries without blurring the whole texture into a grey palette. It excludes cutout margins and neutral white background. One clamped, non-repeating UV field covers each spatial region with filtered sampling. Physical opaque materials with a reflective clearcoat retain colour, lit depth and ambient reflection without tone-map desaturation. Human view projects cached colour from the overhead surface onto the floor along the sunlight direction. This is an efficient filtered-light approximation, not path-traced refraction; the geometry remains fully opaque, while steel and lower supports still cast real shadows. The coloured light belongs to the room preview and is not baked into GLB exports.

**Enter space** chooses a clear spot and restores WASD navigation. Supports constrain movement. The white studio hall starts at 30 m high and extends if needed. Original yellow footprints remain on the axis floor with normal depth testing, so the generated solid occludes them. Make space and Update space retain the axonometric camera, target and zoom exactly; a taller generated roof only extends the hall height.

Human view has sixteen small 3.8 x 1.8 m side-window openings centred 8 m below the roof, curved white side roof panels, six slim roof ribs and clear glazing, 22 real steel grid beams and warm directional sunlight casting shadows onto the floor. One 2048 px shadow map is cached until the scene changes; source textures feather across adjacent material regions in the browser (GLB exports retain each original region map); walking only redraws the visible view. Ten shadow-masked light shafts and 240 static dust particles approximate volumetric sunlight without an extra idle animation loop. Stronger coloured floor light and reflective clearcoat accentuate the opaque space surface. Steel and glass are hidden in axis view. **Sound on/off** controls nearby exhibit audio. The first user click unlocks playback. The nearest model within approximately 4.2 m of its footprint fades in; source model audio also belongs to its generated column at the same grid position. Two audio elements crossfade between exhibits and remember playback positions. Switching to axis view, opening an editing/model panel, leaving the viewport or hiding the tab fades out. Records with an audio association use it directly; legacy compositions can derive one from archive metadata. Audio never requires a new generation job.

**Original models** restores the editable exhibition; edits mark the previous space out of date. **Export GLB** downloads geometry and embedded appearance textures. Original placements are never replaced. A small local recipe rebuilds the active preview after refresh.

The first agent has a 0-50 collage slider. It randomly chooses the Las Vegas-to-V&A or V&A-to-Las-Vegas workflow, combining 5-10 source elements into a dense portrait composition on a 1050 x 1400 board (width:height 3:4). Broad overlapping anchors and smaller accents fill a connected silhouette. It saves editable documents and previews in the local **Collage library** without submitting model generation jobs. Double-click a collage to edit it, or select one or more thumbnails with Windows-style Ctrl/Shift selection and explicitly click Generate selected in the expanded scrolling collage library. Selected model jobs run sequentially and successful meshes are saved with their compositions and media; failed jobs do not create phantom entries. Stop finishes the current model before skipping the rest. **Stop** finishes/saves the current collage and skips queued collages. Inactive decoded images are released between drafts. No new hosted service or paid API is introduced.

The second agent imports up to 50 library models only when the floor is empty. When exhibits already exist, it rearranges those exact placements without adding or removing any model. Large varied square footprints are packed tightly around the physical floor centre regardless of the panned camera target with one full white cell between every pair, including diagonal neighbours. Scale stays near the footprint size (90-99%) and rotation uses 90, 180, 270 or 360 degrees. Selected-model dropdown, button, handle and keyboard rotation use the same quarter-turn steps. A whole proposed layout is validated and applied atomically. Up to 100 one-cell placements can fit with the white gap; an impossible collection leaves the current layout intact. Library contents and floor layouts remain private to this browser/device.

Audio uses varied credible metadata matches; when these are weak or exhausted it falls back to a random archive audio, avoiding the most recent 24 choices where possible. The basis is recorded in linkedMedia. Existing version-1 saved associations are upgraded once on opening while preserving their video, saved camera, model bytes and group. Videos stay muted.

Validation: npm run build; py -3.14 scripts/check_project.py; node qa/verify_canopy_geometry.mjs; node qa/verify_canopy_growth.mjs. The geometry audit checks a single welded closed connected surface for distant supports, finite attributes, retained sizes and unchanged original buffers/materials. Browser validation uses isolated test data and intercepted generation responses with actual GLB fixtures, without running AI inference. The collage-first workflow checks two drafts in both directions, 5-10 elements, portrait aspect and filled silhouettes, no automatic jobs, editable reopening, and one explicitly requested fixture job. It compares camera state and projected floor corners before/after Make/Update space, and checks transparent live-mesh dragging both from the preview and from placed models. Other audits cover layout, audio, atmosphere, geometry and textures.

The accepted shared main and the public site are unchanged. This experiment stays on work/chenyiwei/exhibition-testing until reviewed.

Linked-video metadata updates keep the same player/time when URLs are unchanged. Video arrow keys no longer navigate the model carousel. Network/decode read errors and prolonged buffer stalls have up to three reload attempts preserving the last playback position; Stop/model changes cancel recovery. Proxy timeout after response headers closes the stream rather than appending JSON to video bytes.

Cell indexes and stored placement IDs are unchanged. Each cell is now 2 m wide. World coordinates, occupied-cell planes, dragging targets and original exhibit scale double automatically, including the source height cap (23 m). No copied models or duplicate history records are created. Factory window frames and roof ribs merge geometry to keep draw calls low. Coloured sunlight has stronger saturation and shadow-masked radiance, with one cached 1024 px colour map.

Dragging uses a transparent live WebGL mesh at the source viewing angle outside the floor. Over a valid floor cell, a temporary shared mesh appears in the actual room scene and uses the current eye/axonometric camera. There is no white thumbnail card. Dropping commits the existing placement workflow; cancelling restores the original instance and removes the preview.

Native fullscreen, fixed-height agent menus, Ctrl/Shift collage selection, editable double-click reopening, sequential saves, failure continuation and queue stopping are verified with isolated browser storage and intercepted fixture jobs. No GPU inference is started by these checks.

The browser fullscreen exit control is the only top close icon in native fullscreen. Placement uses bounding-frame move/rotation/resize handles, Delete removal and empty-click deselection. The shared workspace hosts the larger collage library with no pagination. Isolated fixture checks verify these controls and two selected sequential model saves without inference.

The pale axis floor and brighter yellow footprints accompany the new Collage Studio-style page model stage between the room and saved library. Desktop cards are taller in five columns, with single-click opening and no Preview action. Video/audio association, playback, carousel media switching, view overwrite and host transitions are checked with real media and existing GLB fixtures.
