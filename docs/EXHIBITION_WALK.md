# Exhibition Walk

Exhibition Walk is a single 3D viewport of a 40 m × 40 m enclosed white studio hall, initially 30 m high. Walking eye height is 1.65 m. A steel-grid glass skylight casts cached sunlight shadows in eye-level view. Nearby associated exhibit audio fades in after the first click; axis/editing views fade it out. A fine perimeter seam and soft contact shading distinguish the walls from the floor. Open exhibition.html in the local collaboration preview.

The page has no extra Exhibition Walk introduction/title block above the viewport. Click the room canvas to request native browser fullscreen and activate navigation, filling the display without browser chrome. If fullscreen is blocked, the expanded page view remains usable. Native fullscreen uses the browser-provided top exit control only; the custom top close icon is hidden there to avoid duplicates. The expanded-page fallback retains its custom close button. Exiting restores the previous scroll position and disables room navigation and wheel zoom. Esc also exits when no editor or dialog is open. Editors suspend room shortcuts while active. The separate bottom-right Fullscreen button is removed; the browser exit control or Esc exits fullscreen and restores page scrolling. Outside expanded mode the viewport aligns with the archive gutters. Space alone switches Walk/Fly in human view; no Walk mode or Controls buttons are displayed. Two-point and Sound appear only in human view. Make/Update space is the final card below the two agents, using the same heading and plus-button style. Grey preview surfaces use neutral RGB(242,242,242); the architectural room retains its lighting. The axonometric floor uses RGB(242,242,242) against a white background. Occupied plots use saturated #ffd21f yellow at 0.82 opacity, retaining normal depth occlusion. The three preview cards are square and contain the full source composition. The fourth header (04 / AGENTS) uses the same right-hand disclosure chevron as the three previews and toggles all three agent cards together; its state is remembered with the mini-panel preferences. All three agent rows retain a fixed height, including while busy or after space generation. Click the first agent quantity number to reveal the 0-50 slider; the third agent options use a matching floating menu. The sidebar has no vertical scrollbar. A small arrow beside the first agent appears when collages are saved and opens the same large upper-right workspace panel as the three previews. All thumbnails are in a scrollable grid with a right-hand scrollbar and no page-number controls; generation and Stop remain in the fixed footer. Single-click selects, Ctrl/Command toggles selection, Shift selects a range and Ctrl+Shift adds a range. Double-click (or Enter) opens the editable composition. Generate submits only the selected snapshots, one local model job at a time, saving each successful GLB with its original composition and linked media in the model library. Failures are reported and the queue continues; Stop finishes and saves the current item before skipping remaining items. Collage creation alone never submits model jobs.

## Views and navigation

Eye level and Axonometric are separate prominent buttons. Two-point perspective uses a level perspective camera and vertical lens shift, keeping vertical edges parallel when looking up or down.

Axonometric is a true orthographic view of only the 40 × 40 m floor and the exhibited models. Walls and ceiling are hidden. Twenty-one faint grid lines in each direction make 400 cells measuring 2 × 2 m. Left drag rotates, wheel zooms, and right/middle drag pans. Returning to Eye level restores the walking camera and Two-point setting.

In Eye level, click the room to activate navigation. WASD/arrows walk; Shift/Ctrl increase speed; left drag looks around, right drag orbits around the cursor, middle drag pans, wheel moves forward/back, double-clicking the floor moves there smoothly. Space switches Walk/Fly; Q/E change height in Fly. Room bounds prevent leaving through the floor, ceiling or walls. Fullscreen and Esc work in both views. Navigation follows [Enscape's control bindings](https://docs-chaos.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/enscape/pages/842203139/Control%2Band%2BInput), subject to browser-reserved shortcuts.

## Object collage

Two square preview cards align with the top edge of the viewport at the upper right: 01 Collage and 02 3D Model. The counterpart preview is integrated into the first card. Clicking Collage or 3D model opens a larger panel at the same upper-right anchor, covering those cards. A pointer-transparent shade dims the scene and interfaces underneath. Closing retains the composition and model.

Las Vegas objects opens a searchable archive picker with twelve photos per page. It includes only Las Vegas photos with existing isolated-object masks. Local uploads, V&A browsing and whole-photo insertion are not available in this workspace.

Choose a photograph, then click the visible surface of an object. Selection checks the actual alpha pixels of the saved cutout, rather than accepting a bounding rectangle or the entire photograph. The selected object is tinted on the source and shown separately against a checkerboard. Object labels provide an alternate selection method. A two-display-pixel tolerance helps select thin edges.

Ctrl+C copies the selected object; Ctrl+V pastes its transparent cutout into the collage and closes the picker. Copy object / Paste on board buttons provide the same actions. The editor supports move, resize, rotate, mirror, eraser, marquee/group selection, Alt-drag duplication, Ctrl+C/V for board layers, Ctrl+[ / Ctrl+] layer ordering, undo/redo and PNG export. Existing drafts remain available; new additions are verified against the Las Vegas object library.

Generate 3D uses the existing local TripoSR client/backend and the actual target collage flattened from the portrait board to a transparent image. A compatible configured generator is required. Returned models appear in both the interactive larger viewer and the second small preview card. Naming, groups, history and GLB/OBJ exports remain available. The saved model library sits below the exhibition, followed by a full-width 3D stage at the bottom of the page. The final stage fills exactly one viewport without a footer gap. It uses the Collage Studio viewer, typography, orbit/zoom activation, fullscreen, saved-view transitions, carousel, naming and export controls, plus the selected model's muted video and separate audio controls. The existing viewer is moved between this page stage, the small workspace and the immersive room overlay rather than duplicated. Saved cards use four columns on desktop, 4:3 saved-angle previews without a bottom grey margin, and white metadata; the Preview action is omitted on this page, and one click on the image, title or card opens the inline 3D stage. Editing, export and deletion buttons retain their own actions. Cards use three/two columns on narrower displays. Arrows and left/right keys browse saved models and unsaved exhibits, using the existing eased transitions and automatic overview rotation. Click the overview model to enter its saved view. Back to exhibition / Esc returns to the room without losing the working collage. Unsaved exhibits can be saved directly from their retained GLB even after clearing the generator; OBJ export is enabled only when actual OBJ bytes exist.

## Putting models in the gallery

Drag from the second small preview card, or from Drag model into room in the expanded model tools. A transparent live 3D mesh follows the pointer at the source viewing angle; over the floor it appears directly in the shared room scene at the current camera angle. A coloured floor square and cell number indicate the destination. Release over a free cell to place the textured GLB. Materials retain the texture and vertex colours, bypass the room tone mapping and use gentle directional shading with a modest saturation boost, making the exhibits stand out against the white room. Drops outside the floor, behind room walls, over interface panels or onto occupied cells leave the layout unchanged.

The model origin snaps to the cell centre. Its footprint fills 98% of its allocated area. Choose 1, 4 or 9 cells (1 × 1, 2 × 2 or 3 × 3) using the size buttons or minus/plus. Resizing, moving and restoring check the full occupied rectangle, including room boundaries. Proportions are preserved; very tall models stop below the ceiling at 23 m. Both cameras use one shared scene group, so placement, movement and rotation appear immediately in either view.

Double-click an exhibited model in either view to open the shared large 3D model browser. Dragging instead of clicking still moves the instance. Drag the lower move handle on its bounding frame to another floor cell. The rotation handle uses 90-degree steps and the corner handles resize the instance. The bottom-left model toolbar is hidden. Delete removes the selected exhibit; clicking empty canvas or another interface area clears selection. Delete is ignored in editors, input fields, dialogs and the model browser. Fine mesh edges have a two-pixel click tolerance. Camera gestures remain available on empty space.

Each placed copy retains an independent GLB Blob, so changing or clearing the current generator preview cannot remove the exhibited model. The floor layout and unique mesh files are stored atomically in browser IndexedDB and restored after refresh. Repeated instances share geometry/materials in memory and one stored GLB. Removing the final instance prunes its mesh resources. Drafts, models and gallery layouts are local to each browser and are not shared by Git source synchronization.

The making tools use site/exhibition-studio.js, exhibition-picker.js, exhibition-workspace.js, exhibition-browse.js and exhibition-transfer.js. Exhibition-only carousel entry providers leave the original Collage Studio behaviour intact. Run python3 scripts/check_project.py before committing.

The scene renders on demand with pixel density capped at 1.5. Media catalogs/masks load when opening the archive picker, and only the selected photograph's masks are rasterized. The agent creates 5-10-element portrait collages and sequentially generates the requested number of models (0-50). Each intermediate collage is saved before its model job. Manual generation remains available from Generate 3D. Routine checks use an existing GLB and a fixture job response; they do not start GPU inference. This verifies client transport, model display and placement without claiming a newly inferred model's quality.

Local browser checks cover object alpha selection, Las Vegas-only filtering, transparent cutout copy/paste, board transformations, overlapping/dimmed tools, returned-model previews, cursor ghosts, placement in both views, occupied-cell rejection, moving/rotating/removing placed models, independent persistence after refresh and clear, fullscreen and responsive bounds. Navigation and projection/grid checks are rerun after the placement changes. The original Collage Studio and public website are separate and remain available.

Validation covers stage ordering, four-card rows, single-click card opening, unchanged shared viewer count, real MP4 playback with video mute, independent WAV playback, media associations following carousel selection, overwriting the saved camera, fullscreen/Esc, workspace/inline/immersive host transitions and narrow viewport bounds. These checks use isolated saved records and existing GLB fixtures; no inference is started.

## Multi-model selection

In either eye-level or axonometric view, hold Shift and drag a rectangle on empty canvas. The Select button is hidden. Shift adds to the selection; Ctrl/Command click toggles an exhibit. A shared bounding frame provides group scaling, quarter-turn rotation and ground-plane movement. Delete removes the selected exhibits. Clicking empty canvas outside selection mode clears the group. Without Shift, empty-space camera gestures remain unchanged.

Group transforms preserve relative positions and model proportions. Sub-cell offsetX/offsetZ values (metres) are saved with the layout, restored in both cameras, and included in space-generation inputs. Yellow footprints update for all crossed floor cells. Invalid transforms crossing room bounds are rejected as a whole. The three mini editors no longer expose libraries; all saved collages and models are accessed from the lower Saved library; a manual placement selection is available from PLACE.

## Undo, saved libraries and fullscreen entry

Double-click the main canvas to enter immersive fullscreen; one click no longer enters it. The bottom inline 3D stage also enters native fullscreen on double-click. Escape and the browser's top fullscreen exit return to the page; the original exhibition fallback exit is unchanged. The initial/reset axonometric zoom is 1.08. The plan floor is #f7f7f7 and occupied cells use #ffdc57 at 0.78 opacity.

Ctrl/Command Z undoes and Ctrl/Command Shift Z redoes floor edits in either camera. History records one committed move, resize, quarter-turn, group transform, deletion or automatic arrangement per step. It keeps up to 40 undo steps using layout records and references to immutable model Blobs; it does not keep extra mesh copies. Restoring a deleted model reparses its Blob. Layout restoration briefly locks exhibit gestures to prevent overlapping edits, preserves the camera, and saves the restored floor. History itself is session-local; the current floor remains persistent. Text inputs, dialogs, model browsing and collage editors retain their own keyboard ownership.

The collage editor's existing undo/redo now also works when focus is on another control inside the collage panel. Card images use the original 4:3 landscape format and are rendered sequentially only near the visible viewport from stored GLBs using the saved orbit direction and a fitted full-object camera. The isolated preview renderer is removed after each capture; raster reframing is the fallback if a mesh cannot load. A small margin prevents hard edge clipping while preserving proportions. Metadata is white with increased lower padding and spaced buttons. Four columns remain at desktop widths.

The lower Saved library switches between Models, Collages and Spaces. Collages reuse the first agent's editable draft store; deleting a collage keeps its generated models. The current canopy engine automatically archives successful generated spaces as real GLBs with independent previews and camera records. Opening a space uses the shared bottom 3D stage; GLB export and deletion are available on its card, and Save changes updates its stored camera instead of making a model-library duplicate. Libraries remain local to this browser and can be exported. Clicking outside the compact agent dropdowns closes them.

Verification used an isolated browser, an existing textured GLB, and a small fixed geometry fixture for canopy archive capture/export. No inference or production space generation was started.


## Quantity agents, inline editing and playback

The first agent disclosure contains only the 0-50 quantity slider. Plus now composes, saves the intermediate collage, generates one model at a time, saves it in Models and attaches its saved ID to the collage. Failure keeps the editable collage, records the error and allows the batch to continue. Stop finishes and saves the active model, then stops. An unavailable generator disables the action; checks must mock its capabilities/jobs instead of starting inference.

PLACE has a matching quantity slider. Its default pool is the deduplicated Models library; Choose models lets users restrict that pool, and Use all models returns to random library sampling. Sampling uses distinct models when available and independent copies when requesting more than the pool contains. Instances have unique planner IDs even when they share a source GLB. Zero is a no-op. Placement replaces/rearranges the floor without forcing a different camera.

The hall starts in axonometric view on every load. Clicking its embedded canvas activates navigation and manipulation without requiring fullscreen; clicking elsewhere releases keyboard/wheel ownership. Double-click still enters fullscreen. Moves, group transforms, Delete and undo/redo work in the embedded view too.

Models, Collages and Spaces use matching 4:3 previews, four action slots, name/group controls, metadata rows and padding. The lower-left type badge aligns with the metadata left edge. GLB/OBJ exports apply to models, PNG/JSON to collages, and GLB to spaces; OBJ is disabled for spaces. Card rendering waits while the generator, visible rotating model or linked media is active.

Saved-model overview rotation uses model-viewer's native turntable instead of per-frame camera attribute updates. Stopping the turntable converts its current orientation to the equivalent camera orbit before entering the saved view or sliding to the next model. Media recovery keeps playback intent through unexpected pauses and stalled streams, seeks to the retained playback position after reload, respects user pause/Stop/model changes, uses bounded retries/timeouts, and only writes mute/volume properties when they change.

Verification used two mocked model completions with real cutouts, independent repeated-source placement, embedded navigation/delete/undo, default axis after reload, native spin without per-frame camera writes, and actual MP4 midpoint playback plus soft and explicit reload recovery. No actual AI inference was started.
